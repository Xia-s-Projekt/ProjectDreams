# Project Dreams (Working Title)

**Project Dreams** is a powerful, open-source downloader and installer client designed for rhythm game enthusiasts. Built to break down compatibility barriers and region walls, this unified client allows you to download, install, and seamlessly update your favorite rhythm games for both Global (GL) and Japanese (JP) regions—all from a single, beautifully designed application.

*Note: "Project Dreams" is a temporary working title and will be updated as we expand our game library.*

## Features

- **No Region or Compatibility Walls:** Download and play games regardless of your device's geographical region or restrictive compatibility checks.
- **Unified Installer:** Manage both GL and JP region games from one sleek, modern interface.
- **Real-Time Play Store Updates:** We fetch and pull packages directly from the Google Play Store servers. When an update drops, you get it instantly—no waiting for third-party mirrors to sync.
- **Root & Shizuku Support:** Enjoy seamless, background, one-tap installations and updates if your device is rooted or has Shizuku configured.

## Philosophy & Transparency

- **100% Open Source:** Our code is completely transparent and open for anyone to review, contribute to, or fork.
- **No Tracking or Revenue:** We do not track our users, we do not collect personal telemetry, and we do not monetize this project in any way. This is a passion project built by the community, for the community.

## Disclaimer

**Project Dreams** acts strictly as a client and bridge to official distribution channels.
- We **do not** host any APKs, split packages, OBBs, or game data on our own servers.
- We **do not** use, distribute, or claim ownership over any copyrighted assets, imagery, or code from the games themselves.
- All downloads are retrieved directly from official Play Store servers using your device.
